Step 1
Q: Why start Timer in initState instead of build? What if started in build?
A: initState runs once. build runs repeatedly, so starting there creates multiple timers and speeds up the clock.

Step 2
Q: Why does Builds increase once per second, not twice?
A: Each timer tick calls setState once, scheduling one Clock rebuild.
Q: What happens without _timer.cancel() in dispose?
A: The timer may call setState after disposal, causing "setState() called after dispose()".

Step 3
Q: Which is cheap/recreated: Widget or Element? Which keeps State?
A: Widget is recreated; Element persists and associates the widget with its State.
Q: Where is RenderObject and what does it do?
A: RenderObject is in the render tree and handles layout, painting and hit-testing.

Step 4
Q: What is building Tile using ElevatedButton called? Why prefer it?
A: Composition. It combines reusable widgets without unnecessary inheritance.

Step 5
Q: Without keys, why does the count remain on the left when labels move?
A: Flutter matches widgets by type and position, so the left Element and its State stay while the widget label changes.

Step 6
Q: What is the main axis of Column? Why does crossAxisAlignment.center move children horizontally?
A: Column's main axis is vertical; its cross axis is horizontal, so crossAxisAlignment controls left/right placement.

Step 7
Q: Why does Expanded make Text wrap? Explain constraints go down, sizes go up.
A: Expanded gives Text a bounded width. Text lays out within that constraint and wraps instead of overflowing.

Step 8
Q: What happens if Expanded and Flexible are swapped? Which is Flexible with tight fit?
A: The new Expanded button fills its allocated width; the new Flexible button may remain smaller. Expanded equals Flexible(fit: FlexFit.tight).

Step 9
Q: Which is inside the border, margin or padding? When use Padding/SizedBox?
A: Padding is inside; margin is outside. Use Padding for spacing and SizedBox for fixed gaps/sizes when decoration is unnecessary.

Step 10
Q: What determines Stack size? What if icons come before the grey Container?
A: Non-positioned children determine Stack size. The Container painted last would cover the icons.
